    THE METHOD

    Persuasion is not proof.

    Multi-agent systems often sound more rigorous because they produce more discussion. CloudCourt measures whether the council actually changes a decision and preserves the evidence required to inspect why.

    01

    Independent reading

    Each specialist reviews the same sanitized change before seeing another agent's conclusion.

    02

    Policy before prose

    Critical controls are evaluated in code. An eloquent model cannot talk the gate out of a veto.

    03

    Measured disagreement

    The system records whether the full council changes the single architecture agent's initial result.

    04

    Evidence by design

    Every case event includes the previous event's SHA-256 hash, creating a verifiable chain.
